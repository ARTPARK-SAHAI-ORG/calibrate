An annotator is a person who labels items by hand. For the Maths homework tutor, the annotators are two teachers, Meena and Kabir. Annotators do not need a Calibrate account. Each one labels items through a link you send them.

## Add annotators

1. Click **Human alignment** in the sidebar, then open the **Annotators** tab.
2. Click **Add annotator**.
3. Enter the name "Meena" and click **Add annotator**.
4. Repeat for "Kabir".

You can also add an annotator while sending items, from **Add annotator** in the **Assign annotators** window. See [Create a labelling task](/app/human-alignment/labelling-tasks#send-items-to-annotators).

The **Annotators** tab lists each annotator with:

- **Jobs**: how many labelling jobs they have.
- **Agreement with others**: how often their answers match the other annotators' answers on the same items.
- **Kappa (binary)** and **Kappa (rating)**: the same agreement after removing what chance alone would produce. See [How the agreement numbers are worked out](/reference/human-alignment).

Click the pencil next to an annotator to rename them, or the bin to delete them. Click an annotator's name to see their jobs.

## What an annotator sees

When Meena opens her link, she sees the task name, the status of her job and her name at the top. The page has three parts:

- On the left, one numbered button per item. Click a number to jump to that item.
- In the middle, the conversation to label. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move between items.
- On the right, an optional box for comments on the item, and one card per evaluator to answer.

## Label an item

1. Read the conversation.
2. On the "Explains step by step" card, click **Correct** or **Wrong**. For a rating evaluator such as "Encouraging tone", click a number on the scale.
3. Optionally, write why in the reasoning box. If the task requires reasoning, the box must be filled in before saving.
4. Click **Submit & Next** to save and move to the next item.

On the last item left, the button reads **Mark as complete**. Once every item is saved, the job shows **Completed**.

## Change an answer

1. Open the item.
2. Click a different answer. To remove an answer, click the answer that is already picked.
3. Click **Update**.

If you leave an item after changing it without clicking **Update**, Calibrate asks whether to discard the changes.
