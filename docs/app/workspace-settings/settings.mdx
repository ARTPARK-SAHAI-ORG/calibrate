## Open settings

1. Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Click **Workspace settings**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-menu.png" alt="The workspace menu open under the workspace name, with Workspace settings below the list of workspaces" />
</Frame>

## General

The **General** tab has two settings.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-general.png" alt="The General tab of Workspace settings, with the Name field and the Benchmarking choice" />
</Frame>

- **Name**: type a new name for the workspace and click **Save**.
- **Benchmarking**: choose **Parallel** or **Sequential**. This sets how models run when you [compare models on your tests](/app/agents/compare-models). **Parallel** is quicker but puts more load on your agent's server. Pick **Sequential** to avoid overloading it.
