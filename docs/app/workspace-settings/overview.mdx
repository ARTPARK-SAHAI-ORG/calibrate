---

A workspace holds your agents, tests, evaluators and runs. Everyone who is a member of a workspace sees the same things, so a team works in one shared workspace.

You can belong to more than one workspace, for example one per team or project.

## Switch or create a workspace

Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar to see all your workspaces.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-switcher.png" alt="The workspace menu open under the workspace name, listing Maths tutor team and English tutor team, with Workspace settings, API keys and Create workspace below" />
</Frame>

- To switch, click another workspace in the list.
- To make a new one, click **Create workspace**, type a **Name**, and click **Create workspace**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-create-workspace.png" alt="The Create workspace dialog with Science tutor team typed in the Name field" />
</Frame>
