To see the members list, [open settings](/app/workspace-settings/settings#open-settings) and click the **Members** tab.

The **Members** tab lists everyone in the workspace, with their email and role. The person who created the workspace has the owner role.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-members.png" alt="The Members tab of Workspace settings, with the Invite button and one member marked as owner" />
</Frame>

## Invite people

1. On the **Members** tab, click **Invite**.
2. Choose how to invite:
   - **By email**: type one or more email addresses and click **Add**. The workspace shows up for them the next time they log in.
   - **Invite with a link**: click **Create an invite link** and share the link. Anyone with the link can join this workspace.

## Remove someone

Click **Remove** next to their name, then confirm. They lose access straight away. The owner cannot be removed.

To leave a workspace yourself, click **Leave** next to your own name.
