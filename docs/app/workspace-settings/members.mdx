A workspace holds your agents, tests, evaluators and runs. Everyone who is a member of a workspace sees the same things.

## Open workspace settings

1. Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar.
2. Click **Workspace settings**.

The settings page has four tabs: **Members**, **API keys**, **Bring your own keys (BYOK)** and **General**.

## Members

The **Members** tab lists everyone in the workspace, with their email and role. The person who created the workspace has the owner role.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-members.png" alt="The Members tab of Workspace settings, with the Invite button and one member marked as owner" />
</Frame>

### Invite people

1. On the **Members** tab, click **Invite**.
2. Choose how to invite:
   - **By email**: type one or more email addresses and click **Add**. The workspace shows up for them the next time they log in.
   - **Invite with a link**: click **Create an invite link** and share the link. Anyone with the link can join this workspace.

### Remove someone

Click **Remove** next to their name, then confirm. They lose access straight away. The owner cannot be removed.

To leave a workspace yourself, click **Leave** next to your own name.

## General

The **General** tab has two settings.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-general.png" alt="The General tab of Workspace settings, with the Name field and the Benchmarking choice" />
</Frame>

- **Name**: type a new name for the workspace and click **Save**.
- **Benchmarking**: choose **Parallel** or **Sequential**. This sets how models run when you [compare models on your tests](/app/agents/compare-models). **Parallel** is quicker but puts more load on your agent's server. Pick **Sequential** to avoid overloading it.

## Switch or create a workspace

Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar to see all your workspaces.

- To switch, click another workspace in the list.
- To make a new one, click **Create workspace**, type a **Name**, and click **Create workspace**.
