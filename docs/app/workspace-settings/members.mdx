To see the members list, [open settings](/app/workspace-settings/settings#open-settings) and click the **Members** tab.

The **Members** tab lists everyone in the workspace, with their email and role. The person who created the workspace has the owner role.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-members.png" alt="The Members tab of Workspace settings, with the Invite button, Priya Sharma marked as owner, and Kabir Rao marked as admin with a Remove button" />
</Frame>

## Invite people

1. On the **Members** tab, click **Invite**.
2. Choose how to invite:
   - **By email**: type one or more email addresses and click **Add**. The workspace shows up for them the next time they log in.

     <Frame>
       <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-invite-email.png" alt="The Add team members dialog on By email, with meena@example.org typed in and the Add button" />
     </Frame>

   - **Invite with a link**: if the workspace has no link yet, click **Create an invite link**. Copy the link with the button next to it and share it. Anyone with the link can join this workspace.

     <Frame>
       <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-invite-link.png" alt="The Add team members dialog on Invite with a link, showing the invite link and a copy button" />
     </Frame>

## Remove someone

Click **Remove** next to their name, then click **Remove** again in the box that opens. They lose access straight away. The owner cannot be removed.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/workspace-settings-remove.png" alt="The Remove member box asking to remove Kabir Rao from this workspace, with Cancel and Remove buttons" />
</Frame>

To leave a workspace yourself, click **Leave** next to your own name.
