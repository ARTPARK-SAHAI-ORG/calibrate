A simulation evaluator scores a whole conversation in a [simulation](/app/simulations/overview). After each conversation ends, an AI judge reads it from start to finish and scores it against each evaluator you picked for that simulation.

There are two kinds of score:

- Pass or fail. "Explains step by step" asks: does the tutor explain the next step instead of giving the final answer straight away?
- A rating on a scale. "Encouraging tone" rates the tutor from 1 (dismissive) to 5 (warm and patient).

## See your simulation evaluators

In the sidebar, under **Agent simulations**, click **Evaluators**. This page lists only the evaluators that score whole conversations.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/simulations-evaluators.png" alt="The simulation Evaluators page listing Encouraging tone, Explains step by step and three pre-built evaluators" />
</Frame>

Evaluators marked **Pre-built** come with Calibrate:

| Evaluator | What it judges |
| --- | --- |
| Empathy & Tone | How empathetic and fitting the agent's tone was throughout the conversation |
| Goal Completion | Whether the agent helped the user reach their goal |
| Persona Adherence | Whether the agent stayed in its assigned role throughout the conversation |

Each evaluator has three buttons:

- **View** shows the instructions the judge follows.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy you can change.
- **Delete** removes the evaluator from the workspace.

## Create a simulation evaluator

1. Click **Create evaluator** at the top right of the page. The **Add evaluator** window opens.
2. Enter a **Name**, such as "Explains step by step". **Description** is optional and shows in the list.
3. Under **Review type**, pick **Binary** for pass or fail, or **Rating** for a score on a scale. For **Binary**, you can change the **Labels** shown for a pass and a fail. For **Rating**, set the levels of the scale.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/simulations-create-evaluator.png" alt="The Add evaluator window with Name, Description, Review type set to Binary, and the True and False labels" />
</Frame>

4. Under **Judge model**, pick the AI model that does the scoring.
5. Under **Judge prompt**, write what the judge should check.
6. Click **Create evaluator** at the bottom of the window.

The judge reads the whole conversation, so the prompt cannot use `{{...}}` variables. Any you type are read as plain text.

Write instructions the judge can answer clearly from the conversation alone. For example:

```text Explains step by step
Does the tutor explain the next step instead of giving the final answer
straight away?
```

```text Encouraging tone
How encouraging and patient is the tutor's reply? 1 is dismissive,
5 is warm and patient.
```

## Add evaluators to a simulation

You pick evaluators on the **Config** tab of a simulation, under **Select evaluators**.

1. Click **Add evaluators**.
2. Tick the evaluators you want. Your own evaluators are listed first, then the pre-built ones. Click a name to see its model and prompt on the right.
3. Click **Add**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/simulations-add-evaluators.png" alt="The Add evaluators window listing Encouraging tone and Explains step by step, then three pre-built evaluators, with the prompt of Encouraging tone shown on the right" />
</Frame>

To write a new one without leaving the simulation, click **Create evaluator** next to **Add evaluators**.

You can only change the evaluators before you click **Create** to save the simulation. See [Run a text or voice simulation](/quickstart/simulations) for the full set-up.

## How scores appear

On a finished run, each evaluator gets a card under **Overall Metrics**:

- A pass or fail evaluator shows the share of conversations that passed, such as 75%.
- A rating evaluator shows the average score, such as 4.25/5.

The table below the cards shows each conversation's own result. Hover over the small circle next to a result to read the judge's reasoning.
