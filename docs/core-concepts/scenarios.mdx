A scenario describes what the student wants from the conversation in a [simulation](/app/simulations/overview). Who the student is and how they behave goes in a [persona](/core-concepts/personas).

## See your scenarios

In the sidebar, under **Agent simulations**, click **Scenarios**. The list shows each scenario's label and description.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/simulations-scenarios.png" alt="The Scenarios page with two scenarios: Asks for the answer only, and Fractions homework" />
</Frame>

## Add a scenario

Click **Add scenario**. A panel opens on the right. To change a scenario later, click it in the list.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/simulations-scenario-edit.png" alt="The Edit scenario panel for Fractions homework" />
</Frame>

Fill in these fields:

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Label** | A short name you will recognise, such as "Fractions homework" |
| **Description** | What the student wants to get done |

Then click **Add scenario** at the bottom of the panel to save it. When you change a scenario, the button reads **Save**.

### Example scenarios

The two scenarios used across these docs:

```text Fractions homework
The student needs help adding 1/2 and 1/4 for homework.
```

```text Asks for the answer only
The student asks the tutor to just give the answer to 3/5 + 1/5.
```

The first checks that the tutor can walk a student through a sum. The second checks that the tutor still explains a step when the student only wants the answer.

## Best practices

1. Be specific. Put in the exact numbers, dates or details the student would bring up.
2. Give one clear goal. Say what the student is trying to get out of the conversation.
3. Leave personality out. "Gets frustrated quickly" belongs in a persona.
4. Include hard cases. A student who pushes for the answer tests your agent more than one who follows along.

### Scenarios and personas

| | Scenario | Persona |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Says | What the student wants | Who the student is and how they behave |
| Example | "The student needs help adding 1/2 and 1/4 for homework." | "A class 6 student who is stuck on fractions homework." |
