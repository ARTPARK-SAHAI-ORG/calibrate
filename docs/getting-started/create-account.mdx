You need an account to use [Calibrate](https://calibrate.artpark.ai). You can sign up with a Google account or with your email and a password.

## Sign up

1. Open [calibrate.artpark.ai/signup](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/signup).
2. To use your Google account, click **Continue with Google** and pick your account. You are signed in straight away and can skip the next section.
3. To use your email, fill in **First name**, **Last name** and **Email**.
4. Type a password in **Password**. The bar under the box shows how strong it is.
5. Type the same password again in **Confirm password**.
6. Click **Create account**.

## Confirm your email

After you click **Create account**, the page says **Check your email**. Calibrate has sent a link to the address you typed.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/get-started-check-email.png" alt="The Check your email page saying a link was sent to the email address" />
</Frame>

1. Open the email from Calibrate.
2. Click the link in it. Calibrate opens and signs you in.

If the link has expired, the page tells you so. Click **Send verification email** to get a new link.

## Sign in

1. Open [calibrate.artpark.ai/login](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/login).
2. Click **Continue with Google**, or type your **Email** and **Password** and click **Sign in**.

## Reset a forgotten password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
2. Type your **Email** and click **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email from Calibrate and click the link in it.
4. On the **Reset password** page, type your new password twice.
5. Click **Save new password**.

If the reset link has expired, the page tells you so. Click **Send reset link** on that page to get a new one.

## Join a workspace from an invite

A workspace is where your team's agents, tests and results live. When someone on your team invites you, they send you an invite link.

1. Open the invite link. The page says **You have been invited to join** followed by the workspace name.
2. If you are signed in, click **Join**.
3. If you are not signed in, click **Sign in to your account** or **Create a new account**. After you sign in or sign up, you come back to the invite page. Click **Join**.

If the page says **This invite link no longer works**, ask the person who invited you for a new link.

To invite people to your own workspace, see [Teams](/app/workspace-settings/members).
