Calibrate has two ways to learn the app from inside it: the product tour, which walks you through a real test step by step, and the Tutorials page, which has recorded talks and slides.

## Take the product tour

The product tour opens the first time you visit the **Agents** page on a computer. It does not run on a phone.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/get-started-product-tour.png" alt="The Welcome to Calibrate card of the product tour on top of the Agents page, with Skip tour and Start buttons" />
</Frame>

1. Read the **Welcome to Calibrate** card.
2. Click **Start** to begin, or **Skip tour** to close it.

During the tour, Calibrate fills in the forms and clicks the buttons for you while a card explains each step. You click the button on each card to move on. The tour:

1. Creates a demo agent and gives it instructions.
2. Adds an evaluator, which is the check that grades the agent's replies.
3. Adds two sample tests: one the agent should pass and one it should fail.
4. Runs the tests and opens the results.
5. Shows you the failed test, the agent's reply and the evaluator's reasoning.
6. Adds one line to the agent's instructions to fix the problem, then runs the tests again so you can see the failed test pass.

The demo agent stays in your workspace after the tour. You can delete it from the **Agents** page.

You can click **Skip tour** on any card to stop.

## Replay the product tour

You can start the tour again at any time, in either of these ways:

- In the sidebar, under **Resources**, click **Product tour**.
- Click the circle with your initial in the top right corner to open your profile menu, then click **Take a tour**.

Both take you to the **Agents** page and start the tour from the beginning.

## Watch the tutorials

In the sidebar, under **Resources**, click **Tutorials**. The **Learning resources** page opens in a new browser tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/get-started-tutorials.png" alt="The Learning resources page with the Calibrate tutorial recording and slides" />
</Frame>

Each entry has a recording, slides, or both. The page has these entries, in this order:

- **Calibrate tutorial**: a step-by-step walkthrough of every core feature, using a voice agent that fills in forms as the example. Under it is the talk **Evaluating AI systems so they can be trusted**.
- **Convert your AI tool into an AI eval agent**
- **Automatically aligning LLM judges with human experts**
- **Getting started with AI evals**
- **Empowering domain experts with AI evals**
- **AI evals office hours**
- **Evaluating AI products in the social sector**
- **Evaluating and improving AI agents**

The buttons at the top of the page link to the **Documentation**, **Upcoming sessions**, the **Blog** and the **Changelog**.
