This page walks through one simulation for the Maths homework tutor. An AI plays two students, Riya and Arjun, in two scenarios each. Calibrate scores all four conversations.

## Before you start

You need these four things. Each link shows how to make them.

- An agent: the [Maths homework tutor](/core-concepts/agents), or [your own agent connected to Calibrate](/core-concepts/agent-connections).
- At least one [persona](/core-concepts/personas): "Riya, stuck on fractions" and "Arjun, wants the answer fast".
- At least one [scenario](/core-concepts/scenarios): "Fractions homework" and "Asks for the answer only".
- Any [simulation evaluators](/core-concepts/metrics) you want: "Explains step by step" and "Encouraging tone".

## Create the simulation

1. In the sidebar, under **Agent simulations**, click **Simulations**.
2. Click **Add simulation**.
3. Enter a name, such as "Tutor practice sessions".
4. Click **Create Simulation**.

The simulation opens on its **Config** tab.

## Fill in the Config tab

1. Under **Select agent**, pick "Maths homework tutor".
2. Under **Select personas**, pick both personas.
3. Under **Select scenarios**, pick both scenarios.
4. Under **Select evaluators**, click **Add evaluators**, tick "Explains step by step" and "Encouraging tone", then click **Add**.
5. Click **Create** at the bottom to save the simulation.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/simulations-config.png" alt="The Config tab of Tutor practice sessions with the agent, two personas, two scenarios and two evaluators chosen" />
</Frame>

After you click **Create**, these choices are fixed. To try a different mix, add another simulation.

<Note>
  An agent you connect from your own server must pass a connection check first.
  If it has not, the Config tab shows **Verify the connection**, which takes you
  to the agent's connection settings. See [Verify your
  connection](/core-concepts/agent-connections#verify-your-connection). On a
  saved simulation, click **Verify** next to **Launch** at the top of the page.
  **Launch** stays greyed out until the check passes.
</Note>

## Launch a run

Click **Launch** at the top right of the simulation, then pick one:

- **Text Simulation**: the student and the agent type to each other. This is the faster run.
- **Voice Simulation**: the student and the agent speak to each other.

Calibrate has one conversation for each persona and scenario pair. Two personas and two scenarios give four conversations. The run page opens and fills in as each conversation finishes.

<Note>
  Voice simulations work only for agents built in Calibrate. An agent you
  connect from your own server can run text simulations, unless it uses custom
  fields. Text simulations do not work for connections with custom fields.
</Note>

## Read the results of a text run

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/simulations-text-run.png" alt="A finished text run of Tutor practice sessions showing Encouraging tone at 4.25/5, Explains step by step at 75%, and four conversations" />
</Frame>

- **Overall Metrics** has one card per evaluator. "Encouraging tone" shows the average rating, 4.25/5. "Explains step by step" shows the share of conversations that passed, 75%.
- **Simulation Results** has one row per conversation, with its persona, scenario and each evaluator's result.
- Hover over the small circle next to a result to read why the judge scored it that way.

In this run, Arjun asked for the answer only, and the tutor gave it straight away. That conversation failed "Explains step by step" and scored 2/5 for "Encouraging tone".

## Read a transcript

Click the play button at the start of a row to open its transcript.

## Read the results of a voice run

A voice run has the same cards and table. The **Overall Metrics** section has two tabs.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/simulations-voice-run.png" alt="A finished voice run of Tutor practice sessions with Performance and Latency tabs" />
</Frame>

- **Performance** shows the evaluator scores, the same as a text run.
- **Latency** shows how long each part of your agent took, averaged across the conversations:

| Card | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| `stt/ttft` and `stt/processing_time` | Speech to text: turning the student's speech into text |
| `llm/ttft` and `llm/processing_time` | The language model: writing the agent's reply |
| `tts/ttft` and `tts/processing_time` | Text to speech: turning the reply into speech |

`ttft` is the time until the first part of the output arrives. `processing_time` is the total time that step took.

The transcript of a voice run has an audio player for each turn. **Hear the full conversation** at the top plays the whole call.

## Find past runs

Every run of a simulation is listed on its **Runs** tab. Click a run to open it.

## Share or label a run

On a finished run, two buttons sit at the top right:

- **Share** makes the run public and gives you a link. Anyone with the link can see the scores and transcripts without a Calibrate account. Click **Copy link** to copy it, and click **Public** to make the run private again.
- **Submit for labelling** sends the conversations you tick to people who score them by hand. See [Create a labelling task](/app/human-alignment/labelling-tasks).
