Calibrate has two places with material for learning: the Learning resources page in the app, and the blog.

## Learning resources page

In the sidebar, under **Resources**, click **Tutorials**. The **Learning resources** page opens in a new browser tab.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/app/get-started-tutorials.png" alt="The Learning resources page with the Calibrate tutorial recording and slides" />
</Frame>

Each entry has a recording, slides, or both. The page has these entries, in this order:

- **Calibrate tutorial**: a step-by-step walkthrough of every core feature, using a voice agent that fills in forms as the example. Under it is the talk **Evaluating AI systems so they can be trusted**.
- **Convert your AI tool into an AI eval agent**
- **Automatically aligning LLM judges with human experts**
- **Getting started with AI evals**
- **Empowering domain experts with AI evals**
- **AI evals office hours**
- **Evaluating AI products in the social sector**
- **Evaluating and improving AI agents**

The buttons at the top of the page link to the **Documentation**, **Upcoming sessions**, the **Blog** and the **Changelog**. You can also open the page directly at [calibrate.artpark.ai/learn](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/learn).

## Blog

The [Calibrate blog](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/blog) has case studies of real agents evaluated with Calibrate, and posts about evaluating AI agents. Open it from the **Blog** button at the top of the Learning resources page, or at [calibrate.artpark.ai/blog](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/blog).

Posts include:

- [Building a reliable AI coaching assistant for last-mile communities in rural Uganda](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/blog/building-a-reliable-ai-coaching-assistant)
- [Evaluating a form-filling voice agent that enrols mothers over a phone call](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/blog/evaluating-a-form-filling-voice-agent)
- [Calibrate will be at IndiaFOSS](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/blog/calibrate-at-indiafoss)
- [Evaluation is all you need](https://calibrate.artpark.ai/blog/evaluation-is-all-you-need)
